# Continuous Top Five Research

Every completed trading session evaluates the previously frozen five-stock shortlist
against the actual top five NIFTY 50 stocks by close-to-close return. All 50 stocks
remain the ranking universe and controls. A positive return alone is not a hit.

Study Reviews retains each hit, failed pick, missed leader and control. Its Review
field compares the original rank, score, component contributions and feature values
against the selected stocks. Realized gaps, volume and breakouts are kept separately
as information observed during the target session. These describe outcomes, not
proof of their cause. Unverified catalysts remain unresolved.

Study Changes appends research proposals for failed picks and missed leaders. The
report highlights these alongside hits, and maintains cumulative precision by rule
version from complete 50-stock reviews. Duplicate forecast/session/symbol records
are counted once. An absent or invalid prior forecast is never reconstructed as a
forward prediction. Existing forecasts and their rule version are never rewritten.

Each subsequent trading session repeats this process and freezes a new shortlist.
No automated rule promotion is performed: hypotheses require comparison with
non-leaders, chronological validation and forward testing before changing weights.
The report and Sheet findings retain the history and unresolved research queue.

The shortlist frozen on 9 November 2026 targets 11 November 2026. Its first outcome
review can occur only after that session; no result can be known beforehand.
